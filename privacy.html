PRIVACY POLICY

Version 2026-11-01. If we change it, signed-in users are asked to read and accept it again.

In short

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies. We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • You can use the whole site without an account. Your settings then stay in your own browser and never reach us.
  • An account holds your email address, how you sign in, when you accepted our terms, and basic security records. Nothing about what you read.
  • We send no marketing emails. The only emails are the ones sign-in needs (confirming your address, resetting your password).
  • You can download your data or delete your account yourself, at any time, on the account page.

Who is responsible

Crime Diaries is run by madxxenom, who decides how personal data on this site is used. Contact: the contact address. The same contact handles grievances; we acknowledge a complaint within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within 15 days.

If you use the site without an account

We do not collect anything about you. Your browser keeps a few settings in its local storage so the site remembers them. They are needed for the features you choose, are never sent to us, and you can delete them on the account page or in your browser settings:

  • that you read the first-visit notice, and whether you watched the intro;
  • Calm mode, images on or off, sensitive cases on or off;
  • comments and theories you write on case pages, and your votes (for now these are saved in your browser only, not published).

Because we set no cookies or storage that is not strictly needed for what you asked for, there is no cookie banner. If that ever changes, we will ask first.

If you create an account

What we keep

  • Your email address and a password, stored by Supabase. The password is stored only as a secure hash; nobody can read it.
  • How you sign in. If you use Google or GitHub, that service tells Supabase your email address and basic profile (such as your name and picture). Supabase stores what they send; Crime Diaries does not use or show it.
  • When you accepted our terms and this policy, which versions, and that you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Sign-in times (when the account was created and last used), and, for each signed-in session, the IP address and browser it came from, kept for security until the session ends.
  • Short-lived technical logs that Supabase keeps to run and protect the service, under its own retention policy.

We do not ask for your name, date of birth, phone number or location, and we do not build profiles of what you read.

Why

Only to give you an account, keep it secure, and keep proof that you accepted the terms. You give your consent when you create the account; you can withdraw it at any time by deleting the account.

Where it is stored

With Supabase, in its [DATA REGION] data centre. Supabase, GitHub and Google may process data in other countries, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • As long as you have the account. Deleting it removes the account and every record linked to it from our database at once.
  • Sign-ups never confirmed by email: deleted after 7 days.
  • Accounts not used for 3 years: deleted automatically.
  • If our Supabase plan keeps backups, a deleted account can remain in those backups until they expire (7 days on Supabase's Pro plan). Supabase's technical logs expire under its own policy.

Your rights

  • See and download what we hold: "Download my data" on the account page.
  • Delete your account and its records: "Delete my account" on the same page. It takes effect immediately.
  • Correct something, or ask a question about your data: write to the contact address.
  • Nominate someone to act on your data if you die or cannot act yourself: write to us.
  • Complain. Contact us first; if you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Accounts are for adults: you must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly hold data about anyone younger. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. The site itself, without an account, collects nothing from anyone.

AI and your data

We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence, and we do not sell, license or hand it to anyone who would. "Ask the Eye" on the home page is not an AI: it searches our case files with simple keyword matching inside your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Database rules allow each account to read and add only its own records. No method is perfect; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
