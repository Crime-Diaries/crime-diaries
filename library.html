THE LIBRARY

Imported, not reviewed. Entries come automatically from Wikidata and Wikipedia. We include only events at least 10 years old, and leave out mass-casualty attacks on purpose. How the library is built

For education only. Crime Diaries documents real cases to show how crimes are investigated and how evidence works. We do not promote, glorify or condone violence or any crime. Our case files never describe how to commit a crime or show how a victim died. Behind every case are real victims and families. Our principles
